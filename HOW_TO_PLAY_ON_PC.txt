====================================================
  🏁 HILL CLIMB RACING - PC INSTRUCTIONS 🏁
====================================================

TO PLAY ON WINDOWS PC:
1. Extract / Unzip this folder anywhere on your PC (e.g. Desktop).
2. Double-click "Start Game.bat" (or just open "index.html" in Chrome / Edge / Brave / Firefox).
3. The game will launch directly in your browser!

PC CONTROLS:
• D or RIGHT ARROW  : Gas / Accelerate / Tilt Back (Air Backflips)
• A or LEFT ARROW   : Brake / Reverse / Tilt Forward (Air Frontflips)
• SPACE or ENTER    : Start Game / Restart
• ESC or P          : Pause / Resume
• M                 : Mute / Unmute Audio

INSTALL AS DESKTOP APP (OPTIONAL):
When open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, click the 
"Install App" icon (computer monitor with arrow) on the 
right side of the address bar to install it as a standalone 
desktop app on your PC!
